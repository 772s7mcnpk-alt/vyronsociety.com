Why a V

Everything we sell is a count. So is our name.

The oldest record people ever kept was a notch cut into bone: one notch, one unit. A notch can't be argued with. That is the whole idea behind VYRON.

01 · THE OLDEST RECORD

One notch, one unit.

Tens of thousands of years ago, people counted by cutting notches. The first accounting, and the first proof.

ONE NOTCH = ONE UNIT
02 · V IS FIVE

The moment the count closes.

Past four, the eye loses count. So the fifth notch was cut differently: two strokes meeting. Rome kept it: V = 5.

V. VYRON · The vision

The fifth notch. The count that closes.

Four strokes, then a fifth that crosses them all. That fifth stroke is VYRON: the partner that turns everything you spend on marketing into a number you can check.

Our vision

Marketing that can be counted.

We want every business owner we work with to know, at any moment, what each euro of marketing brought back: in customers, and in money. Not likes. Not reach. Not "awareness". A count that both sides can check.

What we believe

Five commitments. V is five.

  1. IThe target comes first.

    Agreed on the kick-off call and written into the contract before anything goes live.

  2. IIEverything is counted in euros.

    Spend, leads, customers and revenue, every month. If it can't be counted, we don't report it.

  3. IIIYou hold your half of the tally.

    Ad accounts, pages, CRM and data stay in your name. We work with partner access.

  4. IIIIOne partner per industry, per area.

    We grow one business in each field and area, never its competitor next door.

  5. VOne team owns the whole path.

    Ads, pages, CRM and tracking, run by one partner from the first click to the euro on your account.

Why "Society"

A society of businesses that don't compete.

Because we take one partner per industry per area, the businesses we work with form a small society: a dental clinic, a showroom, a hotel, an online brand, each the one we grow in its place. Nobody's growth comes at another member's expense.

Where we're going

Local roots. No borders.

01 · BASESwitzerland

Local businesses are our core: clinics, showrooms, hotels, trades and professional firms.

02 · NEXTItaly

The same system, in Italian, for established businesses ready to grow.

03 · OPENWorldwide, online

E-commerce brands, coaches, software and online services, wherever they sell.

IV. FAQ

Straight questions, fast answers.

Didn't find yours? Ask it on the strategy call.

01What exactly does VYRON do?

We plan, build and run everything that brings you new customers: ads, landing pages, follow-up, tracking and reports. You get a whole team, not a freelancer.

02Who is it for?

Established businesses, local or online, that already sell and can handle more customers. Usually from about €30k a month in revenue.

03How much does it cost?

It depends on the package and your goal. We share the price on the strategy call, once we've seen your numbers. Your ad budget is paid by you, directly to the platforms.

04How fast will I see results?

Campaigns go live within 14 days, your first report comes on day 30, and the target is checked at day 90.

05What does the guarantee cover?

If the target we agree in writing isn't hit within 90 days, we keep working for free until it is. It covers our work and that target, never your revenue.

06Is there a minimum contract?

Yes: 3 or 6 months depending on the package, because results build over the first months. After that it continues month by month.

07Who owns the ad accounts and the data?

You do. Everything stays in your name; we only get partner access, never your passwords.

08Do you work with my competitors?

No. We take one partner per industry per area. On the call we'll tell you whether your spot is free.

09What do you need from me?

Answer new leads the same working day, approve ads and pages within 48 hours, and keep the ad budget active.

10Which platforms do you use?

Meta (Instagram, Facebook), Google Search and Maps, TikTok, WhatsApp, SMS and email, plus a CRM and tracking.

11Are the concepts real clients?

No. They're concepts with fictional brands, made to show how we think and work. Real client work is shared privately on the strategy call.

12What happens on the strategy call?

Thirty minutes: we look at your numbers, show you a first concept of your brand and tell you honestly whether we're the right partner.
